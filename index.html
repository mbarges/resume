Meredith Barges


Meredith Barges, MDiv, PhD (ABD)

Bird-friendly Building Policy Researcher & Advocate

LinkedIn/ CV / Bio

I am a bird-friendly building policy researcher and advocate. My research explores how birds have come to be recognized as political subjects worthy of municipal policy benefits and privileges in decisions about how the city is built. My work seeks to reveal how municipal bird-friendly building standards reflect a changing view of the city, from ecological sacrifice zones to biodiverse spaces where birdlife can thrive alongside humans. This has important implications for land use, design/architecture, urban planning, sustainable development — and, of course, human-nature relations.

From 2022-2023, I served as the policy researcher for the Yale Bird-friendly Building Initiative, a collaborative project of Yale Law School and American Bird Conservancy. In that role, I co-authored Building Safer Cities for Birds: How Cities Are Leading the Way on Bird-Friendly Building Policy, the first reoport to analyze bird-friendly building lawmaking.

As a PhD candidate at SUNY ESF, my research focuses on municipal bird-friendly building policymaking in the U.S. and Canada. Combining public policy analysis and environmental ethics, I am seeking to explain why some U.S. and Canadian cities have bird-friendly building policies and not others. In particular, I'm interested in what happens when birds move from being objects of national/state conservation regimes to municipal policy beneficiaries with specific claims on the built environment.

I promote policies to make the built environment safer for birds. This includes pushing for municipal and state bird-friendly building standards, Dark Sky lighting practices, native landscaping, and alternatives to toxic rodenticides. I co-founded and co-chaired Lights Out Connecticut and helped to lead a successful statewide campaign to pass Connecticut’s Lights Out Law (Public Act 23-143), which requires state agencies to turn off all unneccessary lights by 11pm—for birds! From 2024-26, I chaired Lights Out Central New York, a nonprofit project of the Onondaga Audubon Society dedicated to making the night sky darker and safer for migrating birds.

I hold an MDiv from Yale Divinity School and an M.A. in history from the University of Chicago.

I welcome all opportunities to help pass bird-friendly policies. Please contact me if you would like to collaborate.
